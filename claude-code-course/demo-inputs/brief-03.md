# Demo Brief 03 — E-Commerce Brand Launch

*This is a fictional brief for demonstration purposes only.*

---

**From:** Priya Nair, Founder  
**Company:** Bloom & Root (skincare brand, pre-launch)  
**Date received:** [current date]

---

Hi,

I'm launching a natural skincare brand called Bloom & Root. I've been developing the formulations for two years and I'm ready to go to market. I need help with the full brand and digital setup before launch.

Here's what I'm looking for:

**Brand identity**
- Brand name is confirmed: Bloom & Root
- Need a logo, colour palette, typography, and brand guidelines
- The aesthetic is: clean, natural, premium but accessible. Think earthy tones, minimal design, nothing clinical.

**E-commerce website**
- Shopify store (this is confirmed — I've already started the trial)
- Approximately 8 products at launch
- I want it to feel like a brand, not just a shop
- Must work well on mobile — most of my audience will come from Instagram

**Launch campaign**
- I want to launch with some kind of campaign, not just "we're live"
- Open to ideas — could be email, social, influencer, something else
- I have a mailing list of about 400 people from a wait-list I ran

**Timeline and budget**
- I'm aiming to launch in 12 weeks
- Total budget across all of the above: $18,000–$22,000

I've done a lot of the thinking already so I don't need a lot of back-and-forth — I just need someone who can execute well and hit the timeline.

Priya
