# Email Library

Reusable email structures for common situations. The Client Comms agent uses these as reference. Update them when you find wording that works better.

---

## 1. Proposal Delivery

**Subject:** Proposal: [Project Name] — [Your Company]

Hi [Name],

[One sentence referencing how we connected or what was discussed.]

I've put together a proposal based on [what you shared / our conversation]. [One sentence on what it covers and the headline figure.]

[One or two specific things to flag — e.g., an assumption you've made, a question that needs answering, an option you've included.]

The proposal is below / attached. [Specific next step — e.g., "Happy to jump on a call to walk you through it — I have time Thursday or Friday if either works."]

[Sign-off]

---

## 2. Clarification Request

**Subject:** A couple of questions about your [project type] project

Hi [Name],

Thanks for getting in touch — [one sentence on why this project is interesting to you or why you're well-placed to help].

Before I put a proposal together, I want to make sure I've got the full picture. A couple of things would help:

1. [Question 1 — specific, one sentence]
2. [Question 2 — specific, one sentence]
3. [Question 3 — if needed]

[Let me know and I'll have something to you by [date] / Happy to jump on a quick call if that's easier.]

[Sign-off]

---

## 3. Proposal Follow-Up (no response after 5 days)

**Subject:** Re: Proposal: [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

Just checking this landed — wanted to make sure it didn't get lost.

[One sentence on urgency or timing if relevant — e.g., "If you're thinking about a [month] start, it would help to know in the next week or so."]

Happy to talk through any questions.

[Sign-off]

---

## 4. Scope Change Notification

**Subject:** Scope update: [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

[One sentence on what's come up.] This falls outside the original scope, so I wanted to flag it before we proceed.

[Two to three sentences explaining the impact: what additional work is involved, rough cost or time implication, how you recommend handling it.]

Let me know how you'd like to proceed and I'll get an updated scope / variation to you.

[Sign-off]

---

## 5. Project Kickoff

**Subject:** Kickoff: [Project Name] — next steps

Hi [Name],

Great to have this confirmed. Here's what happens next:

1. [First concrete action — e.g., "I'll send a calendar invite for our kickoff call by [date]"]
2. [What you need from them — e.g., "Please send through [assets/access/information] when you can"]
3. [What they can expect and when]

Looking forward to it.

[Sign-off]

---

## 6. Project Completion / Handover

**Subject:** [Project Name] — delivery and next steps

Hi [Name],

[Project name] is complete. [One sentence on what's been delivered and where they can find it.]

[Any handover notes — access details, instructions, what to do if something needs adjusting during the support period.]

[Next step — e.g., "I'll follow up in two weeks to make sure everything is running smoothly."]

It's been great working on this. [One genuine sentence.]

[Sign-off]

---

## Banned phrases (never use these)

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "Please don't hesitate to reach out"
- "As per my previous email"
- "Going forward"
- "Please find attached"
- "Kindly"
- "Leverage" (as a verb)
- "Synergy"
- "Touch base"
