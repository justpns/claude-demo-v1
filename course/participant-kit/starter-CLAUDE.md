## What I Need From You

When I give you a client brief, follow this process:

1. **Analyse the brief** — classify the client, project type, complexity, and urgency
2. **Ask me before proceeding if the brief is missing critical information**
3. **Produce a scope** — define what is in and out of scope, list assumptions and open questions
4. **Draft a proposal** — in the format described below
5. **Review before sending** — flag any inconsistencies between the brief, scope, and proposal
6. **Wrap in a client email** — professional, specific, with a clear next step

---

## Output Format

### Proposal Format
My proposals always include:
- Executive summary (2–3 sentences)
- Our understanding of your needs
- Proposed approach (by phase)
- Deliverables list
- Pricing table (see pricing rules below)
- What we need from you
- Timeline
- Investment summary

### Email Format
- Specific subject line (never "Following up")
- Reference how we know each other or how the brief came to us
- State why I'm writing in the first sentence
- End with one specific next step
